Darkness is upon us. It's that time of year when the Danish weather starts taking a toll on everyone’s mood. There are only two solutions: keep running and stay active, and a whole lot of vitamin D.

Legra started walking which is a bit nuts. Vitto’s back at work and going full steam ahead. It’s hard to juggle everything all the time. But we are slowly (VERY SLOWLY) getting the hang of it.


## Using

**<a href="https://executor.sh/" target="_blank">Executor</a>**: I like experimenting with different coding agents, harnesses and products. But I hate reconnecting all my skills and integrations every single time: Gmail, TickTick, Telegram, Website Analytics, etc. Executor solves exactly that. A one-stop shop for all your integrations over a single MCP. You can use their cloud offering or host it yourself (which I do).

**<a href="https://www.superlogical.com/rex/" target="_blank">Rex</a> by Superlogical**: For the past couple of weeks I have been taking Mitchell Hashimoto's Rex for a spin. It's a nice alternative to Herdr but I would be lying if I told you that it does something magical that Herdr or Tmux can't do. If there is one such thing it's the mobile app! The space of “agentic development environments” is moving fast. Herdr App, T3 code, Paseo, Tern, etc. A new one pops up every week. 

**<a href="https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/settings-and-notifications" target="_blank">Grok Bot</a>**: Speaking of agentic tools. I realized that the annual Cursor subscription I bought a little over a year ago gave me access to Grok Bot. The three features I like the most are the simplicity of the interface, the fact you can have a "master" agent managing all bots, and that every bot has its own Linux VM it can operate. I also recommend watching [this talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L1OBRtfPS34) from Lee Robinson on how Grok Bot is built.

## Reading

**<a href="https://www.theschooloflife.com/shop/tsol-press-on-confidence/" target="_blank">On Confidence</a> by The School of Life**: Saw someone recommend this book online. It's a short but punchy read on what confidence is - and what defines a non-confident person. Great read especially in the age of the “end of work” whatever that might mean. I will be reading more from [The School of Life](https://www.theschooloflife.com/).

**<a href="https://www.chelseagreen.com/product/thinking-in-systems/" target="_blank">Thinking in Systems: A Primer</a> by Donella H. Meadows**: Started this one last week. I had never read anything about systems thinking. So far, it's a banger. Recommend.

**<a href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/huggingface/moe-blogpost" target="_blank">The Ultimate MoE Training Handbook</a> (Hugging Face)**: Everything this team produces I read.

**<a href="https://knlb.dev/essays/surrender.html" target="_blank">Resisting Cognitive Surrender</a> by Kunal**: When working with LLMs it's hard to understand when you're actually learning anything at all. This article articulates that very well and gives advice on how to fight the so-called "cognitive decline".

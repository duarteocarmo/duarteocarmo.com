We've gone from suffering from the heatwave in Europe right back up to Copenhagen. I think I haven't seen the sun in at least 48 hours. 

But not everything is bad. We're back to routines, and children love routines. Legras started *vuggestue*. It's awesome seeing her interacting with the world around her. The flatter terrain has helped my running a lot - and we're back to respectable running volumes (whatever that means). No races in the calendar just yet. 

That's it for your intro. It's cold and rainy - but it's hygge.


## Using

- **<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cargo_bike" target="_blank">Cargo bike</a>** Vittoria and I have a goal. To not own a car for as long as humanly possible. With a child now, our cargo bike has changed how we experience the city. I never enjoyed pushing a stroller. Our cargo bike fits Allegra, a stroller, and Vittoria all at once. We use it everyday. 
- **<a href="https://shop.boox.com/products/go103gen2" target="_blank">BOOX Go 10.3 Gen II Lumi</a>** Some time ago I reviewed the Boox Palma on this blog. But I'm increasingly reading academic papers or long technical reports from big labs. I wanted slightly larger - but easy on my eyes. Stay put for a full review on this website.
- **<a href="https://usgraphics.com/products/berkeley-mono" target="_blank">Berkeley Mono</a>** I spent money on a font. I never thought I would. But spending so much time in the terminal deserves a better font. It's a beatufiul font. And I'm nitpicky on my fonts.
- **<a href="https://meatproxy.me/" target="_blank">Don't be a Meat Proxy</a>** + **<a href="https://dontpastetheai.com/" target="_blank">Don't paste the AI, please.</a>** We are all using AI to work. Some more than others. And that's fine. Some don't want to be in the loop - others want to keep being the engineers that build incredible things. But one thing we can all agree on is that you're job is not to blindly paste AI text. That just means you're not providing any value whatsoever. Send these to the next person that sends you a wall of llm-generated text. 

## Reading

- [Build a Reasoning Model (From Scratch)](https://www.manning.com/books/build-a-reasoning-model-from-scratch) - I'm almost done withis one. Sebastian has a great way of articulating complicated ideas in the simplest of ways. Even though I'd implemented GRPO from scratch before, his walkthrough of the algorithm is unbeatable.  
- [No Bad Kids — Janet Lansbury](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/768148/no-bad-kids-by-janet-lansbury/): Parenting is one of the most rewarding things I've ever done. When you become a parent, you stop judging your own parents so hard, and just wish you can do as good a job as they did. That involves reading about it.
- [Pangram paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14873): Classifying AI-generated text is a cool problem. It's a hard problem - but Pangram appears to have cracked the nut. The paper does not give you every single detail - but the adversarial approach is a super interesting one! 
- [Kimi K3 technical report](https://github.com/MoonshotAI/Kimi-K3): One of the biggest model releases from the past couple of months. Lots of details about how they got this beast done. If only GLM produced reports that were as juicy.
- [How to turn your AI into a world-class designer](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-turn-your-ai-into-a-world) (Lenny's Newsletter): All AI-generated websites look the same. And these days I can even tell if it was an OpenAI or Anthropic model. This post describes a super interesting technique called "seed strings" to inject variety into a design. Bookmark it for your next landing page.
- [Being ambitious and being a dad](https://nicholascharriere.com/blog/being-ambitious-and-being-a-dad/) — Nicholas Charriere: This post resonated with me. Ambition changes. For me, it has just made me sharper. Less time for bs. Less time dedicating to things that have no interest. I've always thought of me as someone productive. But having kids adds a new constraint. You need to cut things. There's no way about it.
